# Research Literature Review: datavidia-11-0

## 1. Literature & Prior Winning Methods
- **Optimal Thresholding & Cost-Sensitive Bayes-Optimal Macro-F1: Lipton, Elkan & Narayanaswamy (ECML PKDD 2014); Puthiya Parambath, Usunier & Grandvalet (NeurIPS 2014); Narasimhan, Ramaswamy, Saha & Agarwal (ICML 2015); Narasimhan, Pan, Kar, Protopapas & Ramaswamy (IEEE ICDM 2016)**: Lipton et al. (2014) established optimal thresholding for F-measure on calibrated probabilities; Puthiya Parambath et al. (2014) proved pseudo-linearity of F-measures and reduced F-measure maximization to iterative cost-sensitive classification; Narasimhan et al. (2015, 2016) proved that for non-decomposable confusion-matrix functionals psi(C(h)) such as multiclass Macro-F1, the Bayes-optimal classifier solves cost-sensitive prediction with cost matrix L* = -grad psi(C*) via Frank-Wolfe / biconcave alternating updates (BEAM-F) over class-conditional posteriors eta(x).
  - *Actionable Techniques:* Replace raw np.argmax(P) with a plug-in cost-sensitive decision rule y_hat = argmax_c (w_c * P(y=c|x)) (equivalently argmax_c (log P(y=c|x) + b_c) with b_c = log w_c). Train base models with unweighted cross-entropy/log-loss to preserve calibrated posterior probabilities eta_c(x), then optimize the 7 class multipliers w (with w_normal=1.0 fixed to remove scale invariance) via Powell/Nelder-Mead or iterative coordinate ascent. CRITICAL LEAKAGE GUARDRAIL: run the optimizer inside nested CV / cross-fitting (fit w on the 4 training folds' OOF or inner folds, evaluate on the held-out 5th fold) and average the 5 fold-learned multiplier vectors w^(k) for test inference.
- **Long-Tail Learning via Logit Adjustment & Effective-Number Reweighting: Menon et al. (ICLR 2021); Cui et al. (CVPR 2019)**: Menon et al. (2021) proved Fisher consistency for balanced error under class imbalance via Logit Adjustment: either post-hoc shifting logits by -tau * log(p_y) or training with L_LA(y, x) = -log(exp(z_y(x) + tau * log p_y) / sum_j exp(z_j(x) + tau * log p_j)). Cui et al. (2019) formalized Effective Number of Samples E_n = (1 - beta^n)/(1 - beta) and Class-Balanced Focal Loss CB_focal(p, y) = -((1 - beta)/(1 - beta^{n_y})) * (1 - p_y)^gamma * log(p_y) to prevent extreme inverse-frequency instability.
  - *Actionable Techniques:* 1) Post-hoc prior correction baseline: compute z_hat_y(x) = log P(y|x) - tau * log(pi_y) across tau in [0.0, 1.2] (where pi_y is fold-training class prior) as a low-variance 1-parameter sanity check against 6-DOF multiplier tuning. 2) Loss-diverse ensemble members: train a neural network (or custom LightGBM/XGBoost objective) with Logit-Adjusted Cross-Entropy (tau in {0.5, 1.0}) and/or Class-Balanced Effective-Number weighting W_y = (1 - beta) / (1 - beta^{n_y}) (beta in {0.999, 0.9999}, gamma in {0.0, 1.5}). Keep unweighted log-loss models as the calibration backbone and blend diverse probability geometries before final threshold optimization.
- **High-Cardinality Target Encoding & Prediction Shift Mitigation: Micci-Barreca (2001); Prokhorenkova et al. (NeurIPS 2018, CatBoost); Pargent et al. (2022); Kaggle Nested/Double OOF Target Encoding Playbook**: Micci-Barreca (2001) introduced smoothed target statistics; Prokhorenkova et al. (2018) proved that standard target statistics cause prediction shift (target leakage within training rows) and introduced CatBoost Ordered Target Encoding; Pargent et al. (2022) benchmarked high-cardinality encoders and showed regularized/mixed-effect and ordered target encodings outperform standard encodings; Kaggle grandmaster practice resolves intra-train leakage via Double/Nested OOF encoding.
  - *Actionable Techniques:* Audit finding in src/build_dataset.py (_fit_oof_target_encoding): precomputed single-loop 5-fold OOF te_* features leak fold-k target information into fold-k's training rows (rows in fold j != k had their te_* computed using fold k), which explains why te_route_klaim_hilang, te_cour_gagal_antar_awal, te_route_konfirmasi_alamat, te_cour_normal, and freq_hub appear among the Top Adversarial Features. Fix: 1) For LightGBM/XGBoost/MLPs, compute Nested (Double) OOF Target Encoding strictly inside each outer fold's idx_tr (splitting idx_tr into 4-5 inner stratified folds) with Dirichlet/m-estimate smoothing, and encode idx_va and test from idx_tr (using matched 4/5 sub-sampling or shrinkage if keeping scale parity for freq_*). 2) Train CatBoost directly on raw high-cardinality categorical columns and combinatorial interaction keys (e.g., route_pair, hub_id+goods_category, courier_id+pkg_speed_class, customer_id+is_cod) using CatBoost's native Ordered Target Encoding.
- **Multi-Table Relational & Sequential Event-Log Engineering: Kaggle Home Credit / AMEX / Optiver / Booking.com Winning Solutions & Parcel Delivery Exception Literature**: Winning solutions on relational multi-table event logs (Home Credit, AMEX Default, Optiver, Booking.com) combine exhaustive entity-level statistical moments and recency/trend splits (last vs historical, slopes, state-transition n-grams, categorical entropy) with hybrid ensembling of GBDTs (LightGBM, CatBoost, XGBoost) and sequential neural networks (GRU/Transformer over padded event sequences). Logistics delay/exception literature further highlights scan time-gap spikes at intermediate sorting hubs, facility congestion/operating hours, and route-segment speed anomalies.
  - *Actionable Techniques:* 1) Sequential scan_events.csv & route_segments.csv engineering beyond global means: (a) Markov state-transition bigrams and rates (e.g., sortasi->transit, transit->upaya_antar, upaya_antar->upaya_antar, pickup->keluar_hub) plus Shannon entropy of event_type, device_type, and moda; (b) trajectory/recency features (first-half vs second-half scan_quality and gap_hours, last-minus-first scan_quality, share of gap_hours in final 2 scans, conditional gap_hours/scan_quality per event_type and per device_type); (c) route-scan cross-consistency (fraction of scan_events hubs present in route_segments.segmen_hub_id, off-route detour count, scan_last_hub_id == seg_last_hub_id, first-mile vs last-mile segment duration/speed ratios); (d) unused dimension interactions from hubs.csv and couriers.csv (ship_hour outside [hub_jam_buka, hub_jam_tutup], hub_luas_gudang_m2 density load, fragility_flag/goods_category vs pkg_fragile_score/cour_sertifikasi_hazardous, flag_alamat_baru vs cust_address_quality/cust_complaint_index). 2) Sequence-aware Neural Branch: train a lightweight PyTorch GRU/1D-CNN/Transformer encoder directly on padded per-shipment scan_events (ordered sequence of event_type, device_type, gap_hours, scan_quality, is_manual, hub stats) + route_segments sequences concatenated with static shipment features, and blend its OOF probabilities with GBDTs.
- **Modern Tabular Deep Learning for GBDT+NN Ensembling: Gorishniy, Kotelnikov & Babenko (ICLR 2025, TabM); Holzmuller, Grinsztajn & Steinwart (NeurIPS 2024, RealMLP); Kaggle Otto Group Winning Ensemble**: Gorishniy et al. (ICLR 2025) introduced TabM (parameter-efficient MLP ensembling sharing backbone weights to output k predictions per sample), and Holzmuller et al. (NeurIPS 2024) introduced RealMLP with strong pre-tuned tabular defaults. Both match or complement GBDTs on large tabular datasets (N ~ 1.24M) and provide orthogonal decision boundaries (smooth manifolds vs axis-aligned splits) critical for Kaggle Otto-style multiclass ensembles.
  - *Actionable Techniques:* Train diverse tabular architectures on the identical locked 5-fold splits (cv_splits.json): LightGBM (fast leaf-wise GBDT), CatBoost (symmetric/oblivious trees + native ordered TE on raw IDs/interactions), XGBoost (depth-wise regularized GBDT), plus a TabM or RealMLP PyTorch model (using QuantileTransformer/RobustScaler numerical preprocessing + periodic/PLR numerical embeddings + entity embeddings for categoricals, trained with cosine LR and cross-entropy or mild logit-adjusted loss). Blend OOF probability matrices via Dirichlet/Optuna/SLSQP log-loss or nested Macro-F1 weights before applying fold-averaged class multipliers.

## 2. Prioritized Experiment Roadmap
1. Step 1 — Leak-Free Post-Processing & Metric Alignment Baseline: Keep the locked 5-fold splits (cv_splits.json) and existing LightGBM baseline probabilities, and implement a cross-fitted (5-fold nested) probability multiplier optimizer (w_c * P_c(x), optimizing 6 free parameters via Powell/Coordinate Ascent with multi-start) plus a 1-parameter Menon et al. (2021) logit-adjustment benchmark (log P_c(x) - tau * log pi_c). Verify per-class F1 gains on nested OOF and check OOF-vs-Test predicted class proportions against true train priors.
2. Step 2 — Fix Intra-Train TE Leakage & Expand Relational/Sequential Features: Replace single-loop te_* (or test side-by-side) with Nested (Double) OOF smoothed 7-class Target Encoding computed strictly inside each outer fold's idx_tr (adding high-signal interactions: courier_id+hub_id, hub_id+goods_category, package_id+fragility_flag, kota_asal+kota_tujuan+moda). Simultaneously engineer missing sequential/domain signals from raw tables: Markov event_type transition bigrams & entropy in scan_events.csv, first-vs-last half trajectory deltas (gap_hours, scan_quality, is_manual), conditional stats per event_type/device_type, scan-vs-route hub detour counts, route_segments moda shares & first/last-mile speeds, and unused hubs.csv operating-hour mismatches (ship_hour vs jam_buka/jam_tutup) and luas_gudang_m2 load proxies.
3. Step 3 — Diverse GBDT Zoo with Native Ordered TE: Train full-convergence LightGBM, XGBoost (hist, multi:softprob), and CatBoost (MultiClass with native Ordered Target Encoding directly on raw IDs/combinatorial categoricals, GPU/CPU) on the exact same 5-fold cv_splits.json. Include at least one diversity variant trained with Logit-Adjusted Cross-Entropy (Menon et al., 2021) or Effective-Number weighting (Cui et al., 2019) to capture distinct minority-class boundaries.
4. Step 4 — Tabular + Sequential Deep Learning Diversity Branch: Train a TabM (Gorishniy et al., 2025) or RealMLP (Holzmuller et al., 2024) tabular neural network on quantile/robust-scaled features + categorical embeddings, AND/OR a hybrid PyTorch sequence encoder (1D-CNN/GRU/Attention over padded scan_events.csv + route_segments.csv sequences fused with static shipment/dimension features) on the locked 5 folds to capture temporal order lost in flat aggregations.
5. Step 5 — Probability Calibration, Stacking/Blending & Final Fold-Averaged Thresholding: Blend OOF probability matrices from LightGBM + CatBoost + XGBoost + TabM/Sequence-NN (via out-of-fold log-loss / Dirichlet weighting or regularized multinomial stacking), optionally apply out-of-fold temperature scaling, and apply the fold-averaged nested class multipliers w_bar = (1/5) sum_k w^(k) for final test inference after passing the OOF-vs-Test class distribution and probability shift gate.

## 3. Metric & Shift Warnings
- 1. Intra-Train Target Leakage in Prebuilt te_* Columns: In src/build_dataset.py, _fit_oof_target_encoding computes single-loop OOF te_* where each fold's training partition contains te_* values computed using the held-out validation fold's targets. Tree models can overfit this intra-train correlation, inflating CV relative to test and explaining why te_route_klaim_hilang (17.00), te_cour_gagal_antar_awal (13.00), te_route_konfirmasi_alamat (11.00), and te_cour_normal (11.00) top the adversarial validation importances. Mitigate via Nested (Double) OOF Target Encoding inside idx_tr or CatBoost Ordered Target Encoding.
- 2. Frequency Encoding Scale & Adversarial Drift (freq_hub): Even with _apply_test_target_encoding's 5-slice random test mapping, freq_hub (15.33) and scan_quality_mean (23.67) show top adversarial feature importance. Audit train-vs-test histograms for scan_quality_mean and freq_hub, normalize entity frequencies to relative shares (rate within fold) rather than raw log1p counts, and verify that dropping or smoothing high-adversarial features does not degrade OOF Macro-F1.
- 3. Threshold Optimization Overfitting on Rare Classes (klaim_hilang 3.03%, rusak_perjalanan 4.98%): Optimizing 6 free probability multipliers globally on concatenated OOF predictions leaks evaluation labels into the decision boundary and risks minority-class collapse or precision/recall overshoot on test. Always evaluate post-processing using cross-fitted / nested fold-wise optimization (tune multipliers on 4 folds' OOF to score the 5th fold), compare against the 1-parameter Menon et al. (2021) prior-shift baseline (tau * log pi_y), and gate submissions on OOF-vs-Test predicted class proportion parity.
- 4. Non-Decomposability & Calibration Sensitivity: Under f1_macro, aggressive sample weighting or Focal Loss distorts posterior calibration P(y|x), which can actually hurt downstream Bayes-optimal multiplier tuning (Narasimhan et al., 2015). Prefer unweighted log-loss (or mild label smoothing / logit-adjustment) for primary models, and only include class-reweighted models as diversity components inside an ensemble whose probabilities are calibrated (e.g., temperature scaling) before multiplier tuning.
- 5. Cross-Shipment Entity Aggregation Leakage & Coverage: Unsupervised cross-shipment entity aggregations (e.g., per-customer_id or per-courier_id mean declared_value or flag_alamat_baru) computed across train+test avoid target leakage ONLY if restricted to scale-invariant moments (shrunk means/ratios, never raw counts due to 1.24M vs 362k split size); any target-dependent or residual entity statistics must be computed strictly via nested OOF inside idx_tr.
